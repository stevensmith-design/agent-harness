# Pack: Marketing

**Status: sketch.** Inferred from the marketing lane of the design pack. Never run as a standalone harness. Treat everything below as a starting hypothesis, not a proven pack.

Build it out when a real engagement demands it — not speculatively.

## What is known

**The verbs:** brand-palette and typography conformance · voice conformance · claims and sourcing check · an AI-tell pass (generic gradient-glow, uncanny stock imagery, walls of bullets) · human read before publish.

**The foundations:** brand — identity, palette, typography, imagery style, and voice *by channel*, which differs more here than anywhere. Plus approved claims and the things that may never be said.

**Starting rung: L1.** "On brand" is a judgement, and marketing is the domain where the corpus pays back fastest, because volume is high and rejections are frequent.

**Web assets are the exception** and inherit the design pack's machinery — a landing page on the product's own domain is product UI wearing a different hat, and gets tokens, validators and review.

## What is unknown, and would need deciding

- **Collateral tokens.** Product has `tokens.json`; decks, social and print need an equivalent machine artifact or the templates drift. Nobody has built one.
- **Whether channel voice is one foundation with sections, or one per channel.** This sketch treats it as sections; at scale that may not hold.
- **Where claims verification actually lives** — a gate, a procedure step, or a human role.
- **Localisation.** Where output ships in more than one language, is a translation a new artifact with its own review, or a derived one? Treat it as a real review lane, not a step, until real work says otherwise.
