# Examples

Three rubrics, chosen to cover three **kinds of standard** — not three domains.

| File | Kind | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `mechanical-data-export.md` | mechanical | A standard measurable today. Criteria are formats, patterns and a declared schema; a gate is writable immediately |
| `structural-commitment-register.md` | structural | A standard about **shape**, not quality. Usually the cheapest gate available, and the one most often left unwritten |
| `judgement-brand-voice.md` | judgement | A standard that is real and **not yet writable**. The corpus comes first; the check may never come |

Read the one matching your standard's kind before writing a rubric. **Do not read all three as a library** — they are teaching material, and the third is the one most people need.
