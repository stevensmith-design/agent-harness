# Rule candidates (staging)

Findings that might become rules land here. **They apply to nothing until the owner promotes them** into the file that owns them and logs it in `changelog.md`.

A candidate needs **two independent occurrences** before it is eligible — three, plus team agreement, where it will bind other people. Once is noise, and a one-off that becomes a rule fires forever on a problem that happened once.

Per candidate: the rule in one line · source and date · occurrences · proposed home · **detectable by** (the grep or validator — prefer a rule that ships with its own check) · status.
