# Trial record

**For anyone trying this kit for the first time. Fill it as you go, not afterwards.**

Three people trying the kit and reporting back produces three opinions. Three people filling this produces three comparable records — and the differences between them are the finding.

**It takes about ten minutes across a session.** If that feels like too much, note that and stop: *"the trial record was too heavy"* is itself the most useful thing you could tell us.

Copy this file, fill it, send it back. **Do not tidy it up.** A rough note written at the moment you were confused is worth more than a clean summary written after you worked it out.

## Notes for whoever collects these

Read the *skipped steps* tables across every record before anything else. A step two people skipped for the same reason is not a discipline problem — it is a design finding, and it outranks anything in the feedback sections.

A record where everything went well and nothing was skipped is the least informative kind, and usually means the trial did not get far enough to hurt. Ask what they were doing when they stopped.

File each returned record under `learning/reviews/` in the harness it came from, or keep them together if the trials were separate — the point is that trial two can be compared against trial one.
