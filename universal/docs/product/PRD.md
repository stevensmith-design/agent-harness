## The problem

What is broken or missing today, for whom, and what it costs them. Written so
someone outside the team recognises it. No solution language.

## Who this is for

The people served, and what they are doing when they hit the problem. If there
is more than one kind of user, say how their needs differ — that difference is
usually where the product decisions are.

## Objectives

Two to four. What must be true for this to have been worth building.

## Non-goals

What this deliberately does not do, and why. The most skipped section and the
one that prevents the most scope argument. A non-goal is not a "later" — say
which it is.

## How we will know it worked

Measures, with the current baseline where one exists. "Engagement improves" is
not a measure; "median time to first export drops below 30s" is.

## Constraints

Regulatory, contractual, platform, or resource limits that shape what is
possible. These outrank preferences.

## Open questions

Product questions not yet decided are rows in `questions.md`, not lines here.
Answering one is a decision, and closes into `decisions.md`.
